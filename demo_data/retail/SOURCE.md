`ledger.csv.gz`: UCI Online Retail II, June to August 2010, built by `scripts/fetch_retail.py`.
Real orders of a UK online gift shop; an invoice starting with "C" is a cancellation (negative quantity).
Cleaned: rows without a customer ID, exact duplicates and bad-debt ("A") invoices removed; five columns kept.
Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online+retail+ii, licence CC BY 4.0.
Cite: Chen, D. (2012). Online Retail II [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CG6D
