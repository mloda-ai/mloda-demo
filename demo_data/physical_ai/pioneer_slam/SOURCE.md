Frames from the TUM RGB-D benchmark, sequence rgbd_dataset_freiburg2_pioneer_slam, seconds 10.5 to 15.8, every 3rd depth frame, ending on the closest approach to the chair.
Depth: 16-bit PNG, 5000 per metre, 0 = no data. RGB re-encoded as JPEG (quality 85).
