Five depth frames of the Redwood "living room 1" sequence, a rendered scene with a simulated depth sensor, as shipped in Open3D's sample data (`SampleRedwoodRGBDImages`).
Depth: 16-bit PNG, 1000 per metre (millimetres), 0 = no data.
Source: http://redwood-data.org/indoor/ (no licence text; the authors ask to be cited). Open3D download: https://github.com/isl-org/open3d_downloads/releases/download/20220201-data/SampleRedwoodRGBDImages.zip
Cite: S. Choi, Q.-Y. Zhou, V. Koltun, Robust Reconstruction of Indoor Scenes, CVPR 2015.
